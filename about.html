Our Story

About Web Cafe

A story brewed with passion, poured with love, and served with a smile since 2018.

The Beginning

Born from a Love of Coffee

Web Cafe was born in 2018 from a simple belief: that great coffee can change your entire day. Our founder, Maria, started with a small espresso cart and a dream to create a space where people could slow down and savour the moment.

Today, we've grown into a beloved destination for coffee lovers and dessert enthusiasts alike. Our menu is a carefully curated collection of recipes perfected over years of passion and countless cups.

We source our beans directly from small-scale farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — building relationships that ensure quality from seed to cup.
